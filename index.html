Donate your items

No appointment needed. Drop off during store hours only; zoning doesn't allow items left outside. Tax receipts available on request.

We gladly take

  • Clothing and shoes
  • Housewares and kitchenware
  • Books, games and décor
  • Electronics from 2020 or newer

We can't accept

  • Tires
  • Mattresses and couches
  • Printers
  • Electronics older than 2020
  • Large appliances
  • Hazardous materials

Thrift with a purpose

2nd Act was started by Milissa West, who brought 14 years of retail management to her dream of a thrift store that truly serves its mission. Revenue beyond operating costs sustains art, cinema and theater programs in Teton Valley.
